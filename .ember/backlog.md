## Proposed features
### Teacher
- Words from Stan: after a conversation Stan adds words/phrases for the learner -> straight into the review queue
- Teacher dashboard: learner activity, streak, most-forgotten words (lapses), checkpoint scores -> focus for the next conversation
- Weekly summary e-mail to Stan
### Learning
- Pronunciation trainer for French speakers: minimal pairs (ui/eu/u, g/k, aa/a, ij/ei, ee/e), listen and distinguish, then repeat and compare
- Graded stories: short Flemish texts built only from words the learner knows (+ a few new), tap a word to see it, add it to review
- Dictation: listen and type
- Personal vocabulary: the learner adds words from daily life
- Grammar reference: every grammar point in one browsable place
- Flemish corner: everyday Flemish vs Netherlands Dutch (ge/gij, goesting, plezant, amai, allez)
